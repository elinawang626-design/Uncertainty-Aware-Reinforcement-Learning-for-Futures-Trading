**Series types.** Individual unadjusted contracts retain both prices on a roll date. An unadjusted continuous series selects one contract per session and can jump at a splice. A back-adjusted continuous series rewrites earlier prices by differences or ratios to smooth the splice. Both continuous variants lack the second executable leg. A continuous-only backtest would be labeled as a proxy.

## Acquisition gate for expanded research

1. With authorized access, quote a small Databento request first. Use db.Historical() and a query with dataset GLBX.MDP3, schema ohlcv-1m, stype_in="raw_symbol", symbols ["ESH5", "ESM5"], and UTC bounds 2025-03-13T00:00:00Z to 2025-03-19T00:00:00Z (exclusive). Request the definition schema separately to check expirations. Inspect minutes and trade records near the open of the March 17 roll. Record query, client version, export ID, quote and SHA-256. CME DataMine can supply the equivalent v2 slice.
2. Map every raw symbol to a unique expiration and year-qualified key with instrument definitions. Compare raw records to the versioned [CME roll calendar](https://www.cmegroup.com/trading/equity-index/rolldates.html) and historical holiday schedules. Establish trade-date assignment, DST, early closes and publication latency. A decision uses the last eligible trade known by session close. A fill uses the first eligible trade near the next scheduled open. Log each leg's timestamp and reject a required leg missing in the opening five minutes. Do not forward-fill or interpolate execution prices.
3. Require both opens on every roll, the incoming contract's close on the preceding session for features, and complete calendar coverage of required active sessions. Verify uniqueness, missing bars, tick increments, positive volume, OHLC bounds, and both prices around each roll. After the pilot passes, retrieve the full panel, version the schedule, record retrieval date, coverage and hashes, and repeat the gate.
4. Keep licensed data local. Archive hashes, instrument IDs, code and research outputs allowed by the agreement. Keep any ledger that reconstructs licensed prices local unless the agreement permits sharing.

No full archive was downloaded. The [holdout protocol](es-holdout-protocol.md) defines the next experiment before observing its performance.

## March 2025 roll pilot

The pinned public ES sample includes [per-contract minute Parquets](https://huggingface.co/datasets/lynx1231/historical-futures-data-sample/tree/29efdfa21c5a5b2d7aa306397385cf116e011559/data/minute/CME/ES). Both files match the SHA-256 digests in the revision's inventory. The first observed minute label for the March 17 roll is Sunday, March 16, at 17:00 Chicago time (22:00 UTC) for both legs. The ESH25 bar opens at 5626.75 and the ESM25 bar at 5678.75, matching their pinned daily opens. The ESM25 March 14 final observed minute closes at 5681.00. These bars establish minute-level overlap only. The publisher's `trading_date` column labels the first roll bars March 16, so CME sessions must be assigned from the calendar. The bars lack individual trade times and feed receipt times, and the sample covers only five ES contracts.

The [Historical Futures Data public ES 10-second sample](https://historicalfuturesdata.com/browse/ES) was also downloaded and checked for schema. It has 164,895 aggregated records and no contract identifier. The vendor advertises separate paid tick files by contract. Their completeness, historical receipt semantics and licensing for this research remain unverified.

For a point-in-time pilot, use Databento `GLBX.MDP3`, raw symbols `ESH5` and `ESM5`, schemas `definition` and `trades`, and the UTC range `[2025-03-13T00:00:00Z, 2025-03-19T00:00:00Z)`. `trades` supplies exchange and capture-server timestamps. In the vendor portal, quote the two schemas separately and export DBN, or run the helper with your API key set in your local environment. [Databento Trades schema](https://databento.com/docs/schemas/trades) · [Portal guide](https://databento.com/docs/portal/batch-download)

```bash
python -m pip install databento
python -m src.es_databento_pilot
# Set PILOT_BUDGET_USD to a cap you accept after seeing both quotes.
python -m src.es_databento_pilot --max-usd "${PILOT_BUDGET_USD:?set an approved USD cap}"
```

The first command that invokes the module uses the free metadata quote API. The download requires an explicit estimate cap and a fresh output directory, by default under the ignored `data/raw/databento/es-roll-pilot/`. The manifest includes the query, quotes and hashes for completed files. Keep licensed raw data outside Git. The downloaded pilot must still pass the acquisition gate.

Databento's July 2010 to May 2017 backfill lacks capture-server receipt times. The vendor populates both timestamp fields with CME FIX SendingTime and flags the receive timestamp. Under the current holdout protocol, establish a documented point-in-time availability guarantee or find an alternate source for that period before qualifying it. An alternate start date or availability rule would require a dated protocol amendment before performance is inspected. ## ES price and session validation

For the March 12, 2025 trade date, the pinned ESH25 bar and the CME sample both open at 5580.00. The provider reports 5604.75 in its close column, the same as the reported settlement. The sample's last qualifying trade is 5597.25. The prior session policy and date-only bars are retained for reproducing the reference diagnostic. The reference manifest records those limitations explicitly. [CME sample](https://www.cmegroup.com/files/download/xcme-tick-es-fut-20250312-part-00039-c000.csv) · [Settlement reported March 13](https://bluelinefutures.com/2025/03/13/e-mini-sp-and-nq-futures-hold-steady-ahead-of-ppi-and-jobless-claims/)

For new data, call `src.prep_day1.build_es(contracts, calendar, rolls, first)` after independently constructing the historical calendar and aggregating eligible trades. Supply:

- A calendar indexed by unique, increasing, timezone-naive CME trade dates, with timezone-aware `session_open` and `session_close` columns. Include the warm-up and final exit sessions, with historical early closes and holidays accounted for.
- A mapping of year-qualified contract symbols to bars indexed by the same trade dates. Include `open`, `close`, `volume` and the timezone-aware `open_at`, `close_at` and `close_available_at` columns. Derive `open_at` from the first eligible trade and `close_at` from the last eligible trade. Obtain `close_available_at` from observed feed availability or a documented point-in-time feed guarantee.
- Dated roll mappings and the initial active symbol. The builder checks both roll legs and the incoming contract's preceding close. Each required open must occur within five minutes of its scheduled session open. Each close must be available by its scheduled session close. Missing required dates, prices, or timestamps raise an error. The output records the decision, entry and exit timestamps. On a roll the outgoing exit and incoming entry are recorded in adjacent rows.

Timestamp checks validate the supplied records against the supplied calendar. Source qualification still requires verifying feed conventions, the trade selection, and the calendar itself. The existing `src.experiment` command runs the pinned reference diagnostic.
